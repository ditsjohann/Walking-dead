== a bat to the body of a walker on its last legs (3 health): it lives (expect health 1.0 or more)
item replace entity Bot weapon.mainhand with outbreak:baseball_bat
summon outbreak:walker 0 -57 2.5 {Tags:["last"],PersistenceRequired:1b,Rotation:[180f,0f],Health:3f}
